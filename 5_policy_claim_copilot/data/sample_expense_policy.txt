# ACME CORP TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY (2026)

## 1. MEAL REIMBURSEMENTS
- Employees are eligible for meal reimbursement during business travel.
- Daily Maximum Allowances:
  * Breakfast: Up to $25 per day.
  * Lunch: Up to $35 per day.
  * Dinner: Up to $60 per day.
- Daily Total Cap: Total meal expenses must not exceed $120 per day.
- Itemized Receipts: An itemized receipt showing individual food items and costs is mandatory for any expense over $25. Credit card charge slips alone are not acceptable.

## 2. ALCOHOL & PERSONAL EXPENSES
- Alcohol is strictly non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized client entertainment dinner with pre-approval from a Vice President.
- Personal items (toiletries, hotel pay-per-view, minibar snacks, souvenirs) will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.

## 3. LODGING & ACCOMMODATION
- Standard business hotel rooms up to $250 per night (excluding taxes) in standard tier cities.
- High-cost metro areas (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo) allow up to $350 per night.
- Bookings must be made via the corporate travel portal at least 14 days in advance.

## 4. TRANSPORTATION
- Flights: Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium Economy allowed for long-haul international flights over 6 hours.
- Rideshare / Taxis: Uber, Lyft, or local licensed taxis are covered for transit between airport, hotel, and client sites. Luxury or Black car tiers are strictly non-reimbursable.
- Rental Cars: Intermediate or standard car tier only. Personal insurance add-ons will not be reimbursed as corporate umbrella insurance applies.

## 5. SUBMISSION DEADLINES
- All claims and itemized receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
- Late submissions will be rejected automatically by Finance.
